Prototype flows and recovery branches
Eight flows · 47 steps · 23 named recovery exits
Each step names its screen, its state, and what the operator can safely leave
How to read a flow
Each numbered step names the screen it happens on (page 04 or 05), the state that screen is in, and the decision available. Amber blocks are recovery branches — not error handling bolted on, but paths the product expects to take.
Two rules every flow obeys
Nothing publishes itself, and nothing traps the operator. Every long step states that leaving is safe and reports back through the job tray; every decision step can be deferred without losing work.
Where flows end
Not on a success screen. Each flow ends on the updated state it changed — the record, the health vector, the cost line — and, where relevant, on the next blocker. There is no congratulation step in this product.
