Laptop, tablet, and responsive behaviour
1440 primary · 1280 laptop proof · 1024 tablet proof · 390 read-only
Some tasks stay desktop-only, and the design says why
The rule
Corpus is desktop-first because governed review needs two things side by side. Narrowing never removes information — it changes how information is reached. Columns become a second line, panes become drawers, and matrices become lists.
The limit
Three surfaces are desktop-only: duplicate comparison, rights and takedown, and the reprocess composer. On a narrower screen they open read-only with a stated reason — not a squeezed version that makes a consequential decision feel casual.
