Relationship to the App system
Same ink, same green, same Plex family, same 4-base spacing. Three deliberate divergences: the surface family is cool Mist-derived rather than Warm Paper, the display face (Bricolage) is not used at all, and status colour is split into eight axis-specific families instead of one three-step risk scale.
Why cool surfaces
Warm Paper is the learner's study surface and carries the App's welcome. Corpus is an instrument: the operator reads dense evidence for an hour a week, and warm cream under twelve columns of tabular data reads as fatigue. Mist keeps the family resemblance and lets amber and plum stay legible.
Contrast policy
Every text token meets 4.5:1 on its intended surface; graphic tokens meet 3:1. Two hues per family — a graphic hue for dots, bars and borders, and a darker text hue for words. Using a graphic hue as text is a defect, not a preference.

Faces and licences
IBM Plex Sans 400/500/600 — all UI text and all numbers. IBM Plex Mono 400/500/600 — identifiers, URLs, hashes, versions, eyebrow labels, state chips. Both SIL OFL 1.1, self-hostable, no display face in Corpus.
Chosen for German behaviour: unambiguous Il1 0O, full umlaut and ß coverage at 12px, and true tabular figures so 47 231 columns align without manual width juggling. Fallback stack: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.
font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums — set on body, never per component.

01.6
Icons, motion, and visualization
Icons
A 22-glyph Lucide subset (ISC licence, self-hosted, tree-shaken). 1.75px stroke on a 24 grid; rendered at 16 (inline), 20 (control), 24 (rail). Minimum hit target 32×32 in dense rows, 44×44 on tablet.

Rules: an icon depicts the noun or verb it stands for; a geometric marker in a chip is a marker, not an icon; status icons always sit beside their word and are aria-hidden; no emoji; nothing celebratory.
