The console this design describes
One operator, one prioritized inbox, and evidence that never pretends to be more certain than it is.
Corpus turns provenance-preserving German A2/B1 exam evidence into rights-aware, deduplicated exam intelligence for Bestehen / App. The console exists so ordinary maintenance never needs database surgery — and so no maintenance action can quietly collapse provenance, confidence, rights, difficulty, QA readiness, or content independence into one green tick.

Deliberately absent
No learner accounts, attempts, Readiness, coaching, practice delivery or progress. No public corpus browser, no source mirror, no public API console, no submissions from anyone but the owner. No roles, assignments, approvals, comments, SLA clocks, billing, or collaboration feed. No enterprise BI. Each of these is either App-owned or explicitly deferred by §7.2 of the specification; designing a surface for it would imply a commitment the product has not made.
No universal “production ready” switch · no scalar Corpus health score · no generic “Retry everything” · no generic Delete
