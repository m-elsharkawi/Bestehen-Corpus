## Compact project context

Corpus is an exam-data/intelligence product for broad German A2/B1 evidence. Bestehen / App is its learner-facing downstream customer and owns learner state, coaching, Readiness and original exercise generation/evaluation. Corpus is initially operated by one owner as a personal project.

Architecture must preserve six independent truth axes: provenance; authenticity/confidence; rights/usage; difficulty plus Generic-B1/TELC relevance; extraction/QA capability readiness; identity/derivative lineage/metric-specific independence. Unknown/disputed/provisional/local states are first-class and there is no universal readiness flag. Ordinary App sessions must not require live Corpus inference. All external input is untrusted. Prefer cheap precomputed/batchable paths with bounded escalation.

## Product/design truths not to reopen

Corpus is not a learner course, public mirror, generic scraper, learner-attempt store or exam authority. Launch excludes public submissions/API/research UI, multi-tenancy, billing, collaboration and authenticated paid/private crawling. Owner console is internal. Do not let trust zones collapse six axes.
