## Decisions this stage owns

Settle every Stage 07 checklist item and at minimum:

1. **Provenance graph.** Define durable relationships from discovery candidate/location → Source Observation → fetched artifact/version → normalized/extracted records, preserving redirects, mirrors, detached assets/keys and evidence references.
2. **Authenticity/confidence state model.** Define A/B/C/D/review/quarantine semantics and how confidence dimensions are represented separately from the overall tier.
3. **Local scope.** Define source/document/exam/task/item/asset-level confidence and inheritance rules. Inherited values must be distinguishable from local observations and unknown remains in the denominator where product rules require it.
4. **Evidence dimensions.** Map provider reputation, structural similarity, language quality, answer-key presence/consistency, trusted-material similarity, internal consistency and independent corroboration to architecture-owned evidence inputs without hardcoding mutable calibration weights.
5. **Corroboration independence.** Consume Stage 06 observations and future Stage 16 identity/independence without treating URL/host count as support count. Define provisional behavior before Stage 16 resolves clusters.
6. **Conflicting evidence.** Define how conflicting answer keys, claimed-official labels, structural contradictions and weak provenance create reviewable evidence rather than destructive overwrite.
7. **Decision lineage.** Every manual/automated trust decision carries evidence references, rule/method/model version, actor, time and supersession/review lineage.
8. **Influence gates.** Architecture must support separate eligibility for exploratory structure, difficulty input, learning-priority influence, TELC-specific claims, direct App surfacing and other product uses; do not implement one trust threshold.
9. **Reassessment/reprocessing.** New evidence may supersede a confidence decision without erasing historical observations or silently changing downstream published artifacts.
10. **Operations/query needs.** Support design workbench dimensions, corroboration view, contradicting evidence, downstream-use effects and rationale-required decisions without a special backend per screen.

## Alternatives / simplicity / cost

Compare an append-only evidence graph/projection model with a single mutable trust score. Reject complexity that adds a graph database/search service unless Stage 05 evidence proves PostgreSQL cannot satisfy the needed relationships/querying. Trust calculation should be deterministic/rule-based where possible; model assistance is a candidate input, not authority.

Include an **App implementation reuse assessment**. Expected default: current relational/runtime stack is sufficient and no new paid service/account is needed.

## Failure, security and testing

Define missing/corrupt provenance link, contradictory evidence, stale source, unresolved mirror lineage, rule-version migration and bad model-candidate behavior. Source text/model output cannot execute tools or grant authority. Define property/invariant tests for no trust→rights side effect, no mirror inflation, locality, history/supersession and deterministic replay.
