## Product invariants

Q0–Q3 findings are explicit, scoped and evidence-linked. Capability eligibility is independent across usages; no universal `production_ready` flag. Trust, rights, difficulty/relevance and independence remain separate axes. Parent status cannot erase local defects. Technical/provider failure is not content failure. Historical findings/decisions remain auditable under rule changes.

## Mandatory reads

## Decisions this stage owns

1. Define Finding identity, scope, severity/class Q0–Q3, rule/check version, evidence refs, status, owner action, suppression/resolution and supersession.
2. Define deterministic validation layers from structural integrity through answer correctness, content quality and use-specific publication criteria.
3. Define capability families and eligibility gates separately for analytics, internal reference, difficulty/relevance, pattern publication, exact content/media use and other product-approved uses.
4. Define how authenticity/confidence, rights, extraction quality, answer certainty and independence feed gates **without collapsing into one score**.
5. Define blockers/warnings/review-needed/unknown semantics and local parent-child propagation.
6. Define validation under partial/incomplete exams and assets: safe capabilities may publish while affected ones remain held.
7. Define correction/revalidation behavior after owner decisions, new source evidence, rights changes, taxonomy migration, dedup changes or processor version changes.
8. Define automated-vs-human QA boundaries and benchmark promotion gates; model output cannot self-certify.
9. Define publication eligibility snapshots with exact rule/taxonomy/data revisions for Stage 19.
10. Define health/reporting queries and design state rendering without a backend fork per capability.
11. Define audit requirements for overrides, suppressions and material eligibility changes.

## Alternatives / cost
