## 3. Product and Design Invariants Every Architecture Stage Must Respect

Every generated stage prompt must repeat the subset relevant to that stage and link it to the owning product/design sources.

### 3.1 Product boundary

- Corpus is an **exam-data and intelligence product**, not a learner-facing course, public mirror, generic scraper, learner-attempt store, or certification/scoring authority.
- Initial acquisition covers German A2, B1, and mixed A2–B1 sources; A1 scaffolding belongs to App.
- TELC B1 is the strongest target authority signal, not the only source family.
- Primary customer is Bestehen / App; the single owner/operator is the initial human user.
- Launch excludes public submissions/API/research UI, multi-tenancy, billing, collaboration workflows, authenticated crawling of paid/private third-party sites, and enterprise controls.

### 3.2 The six axes must never collapse

Architecture must model separately:

1. Source/provenance.
2. Authenticity/evidence confidence.
3. Rights/usage.
4. Difficulty and Generic-B1/TELC relevance.
5. Extraction/QA readiness by capability.
6. Content identity, derivative lineage, and metric-specific analytical independence.

No table, aggregate, API DTO, search index, cache, UI state, or publication flag may erase these distinctions.

### 3.3 Uncertainty and local evidence

- `unknown`, `not_applicable`, `disputed`, `review_required`, `unresolved_dependency`, `incomplete`, and `provisional` are first-class states.
- Missing values are never invented to satisfy a schema.
- The smallest relevant scope controls high-risk uses.
- A trusted parent does not rescue a defective child.
- One local defect does not invalidate unaffected parent/sibling capabilities unless evidence shows broader corruption.

### 3.4 Capability-scoped publication

- There is no universal `production_ready=true`.
- Promotion/eligibility is per stated capability with remaining restrictions and versions.
- Structure analytics, correctness/scoring, taxonomy, abstract pattern intelligence, exact source reuse, asset exposure, and independent prevalence can have different gates.
- Quarantine, hold, limited/provisional, stale, superseded, and invalidated states must propagate predictably.

### 3.5 Provenance and canonical model

- Preserve Source Observation → Document → Exam → Section → optional Part → Task → Item.
- Instructions, Stimuli, Options, Answers/Scoring, Assets, provenance observations, derivations, findings, decisions, and versions are first-class where the product specifies them.
- Observed source expression remains distinct from curated interpretation/correction and derived intelligence.
- Stable identity and mutable revision/version semantics must survive reprocessing and contract publication.

### 3.6 Trust, rights, and governance

- Officiality/authenticity/confidence and usage rights are independent.
- Unknown rights never imply permission.
- Allow/block acquisition policy grants neither confidence nor rights.
- Manual corrections/overrides preserve old state and remain authoritative across reruns until explicitly superseded.
- Consequential human/automated changes are auditable with actor, time, old/new state, reason/evidence, method/model/rule/taxonomy versions, and downstream impact.
- Takedown/security blocks override ordinary acquisition/reprocessing paths and prevent mirror re-ingestion where required.

### 3.7 Deduplication and analytics

- Preserve all observations while separating content identity, derivative lineage, canonical provenance anchor, preferred processing representation, and metric-specific independence.
- Mirrors/derivatives cannot manufacture prevalence, corroboration, or diversity.
- Decision-grade results carry denominator, independent evidence, confidence/uncertainty, source/provider mix, version, freshness, and applicability.
- Raw/source volume is not decision-grade evidence weight.

### 3.8 Corpus → App boundary

- App requests desired exam/learning patterns, not learner profiles.
- Corpus returns eligible structured intelligence, constraint blueprints, governance envelopes, versions, and change impact; it does not decide learner coaching or Readiness.
- Original practice generation/evaluation belongs to App. Source content is direct-delivery input only through explicit rights-governed capability.
- Corpus stores no learner data.
- App sessions do not trigger routine Corpus AI inference; intelligence is precomputed, cached, batched, and reusable.

### 3.9 Pipeline and recovery

Preserve the conceptual lifecycle:

**Discover → Fetch → Normalize → Parse → Segment → Classify → Extract Answers → Extract Assets → Enrich → Validate → Deduplicate → Review → Publish**

Architecture may implement it differently, but must preserve:

- partial success at the smallest meaningful scope;
- idempotent/replayable work;
- method/input/model/rule/taxonomy/version lineage;
- explicit retry/defer/review/quarantine/hold states;
- scoped non-destructive reprocessing from the earliest affected stage;
- curator-override conflict handling;
- downstream stale/recompute/invalidate propagation;
- bounded retry and no expensive failure loops.

### 3.10 Security and privacy

- Corpus has a near-zero learner/personal-data footprint.
- Every external input is untrusted and never executed as active content.
- Security preflight precedes deep/expensive processing.
- Archives, redirects, network destinations, file size/type, HTML/rich content, embedded assets, OCR/ASR/model inputs, and prompt injection require bounded handling.
- Source text cannot command tools, publishing, policies, or secrets.
- Operator authority, credentials, and secrets are isolated from source data.
- Retention/deletion must preserve legitimate provenance/audit/derived truth while honoring takedown/security/privacy requirements.

### 3.11 Benchmark and quality

- No automated production capability claims qualification without an instantiated benchmark/gold core and relevant gates.
- Stage 16 specifies a 16-slot seed, 12–14-package gold core, content-family-disjoint 70/30 development/holdout split, negative/uncertain cases, immutable releases, and incident replays.
- `corpus-benchmark-1.0.0` is a bootstrap obligation, not an already-existing artifact.
- Algorithms, models, thresholds, and vendors remain replaceable subject to benchmark qualification.

### 3.12 Cost and scale

- Design center: roughly 20k–50k canonical Items, not hypothetical internet scale.
- Product target: at most approximately `€3 / 1,000 Items` variable processing under the defined measurement policy and approximately `€25/month` normal Corpus cash envelope, subject to later measured recalibration.
- Human review is a cost even without an invoice.
- Prefer deterministic/local/open-source first, content-addressed reuse, batching, caching, deferred enrichment, bounded crawling, and risk-triggered escalation.
- Premium services are invoked only for defined high-impact ambiguity or failed cheaper gates, not every source/view/item.
- Architecture must measure cost by productive/qualified output and failed/retried work, not only vendor invoices.

### 3.13 Operations and design

- Launch needs a minimal owner-only operations surface, not raw database surgery and not enterprise workflow.
- One prioritized Inbox with specialized views, reusable record/review detail, Source Controls & Intake, scoped Reprocessing & Change Actions, and Corpus Health are the core areas.
- Health is a vector; no scalar score can hide Q0/Q1, rights, quarantine, correctness, or regression blockers.
- Complex comparison/review is desktop-first; responsive/accessibility behavior must match the design handoff.
- Architecture must support design states without embedding a specific design-tool runtime.

## 6. Checklist Design Requirements

For every stage section, include:

5. **Research/evidence** — current official sources or benchmark work required.
6. **Alternatives and rationale** — options compared and chosen default.

13. **Output and downstream handoff** — exact Markdown path and consumers.

## 9. Cost-Control Requirements for the Prompt Pack

Architecture must preserve Corpus quality without designing an enterprise platform or an always-on AI bill.

Every cost-sensitive stage prompt must require:

- workload assumptions tied to the low/base/high product envelopes;
- recurring cash, variable processing, storage/egress, human-review, and maintenance-time costs;
- cost per productive/qualified Item or capability output;
- retry/failure/reprocess cost visibility;
- free/cheap default and upgrade trigger;
- caching/content-addressed reuse/batching/deferred work strategy;
- spending caps or paid-job gates where the product requires them;
- current official pricing research without inventing permanent prices.

Preferred default pattern:

1. deterministic rule/parser/hash/metadata work;
2. local/open-source processing where operationally sane;
3. cached/batched inexpensive managed capability;
4. selective stronger model/service;
5. human review for high-impact ambiguity/gold truth.

Reject by default unless evidence justifies:

- premium AI/OCR/ASR on every source/item;
- live model inference for ordinary App requests or operations-page loads;
- unbounded crawling, archive expansion, retries, or backfills;
- full-video understanding where audio/captions/selected frames suffice;
- multiple specialized managed services before measured need;
- microservices per domain in a one-owner project;
- premature lakehouse/warehouse/streaming platform/vector database;
- duplicate storage/processing without content-addressed reuse;
- high-scale infrastructure for hypothetical millions of records;
- vendor lock-in without export/replay/benchmark fallback.

Paying more is justified for current target correctness, benchmark/gold truth, unique high-value evidence, rights/takedown/security handling, or measured labor/reliability savings that exceed the added cost.

## 10. Security, Privacy, Rights, and Safety Requirements

The prompt pack must force security to appear early in intake/pipeline design and later as an end-to-end threat-model/release concern.

Require:

- data-flow and trust-boundary diagrams;
- untrusted-input policy before parsing/model processing;
- SSRF/redirect/DNS/egress protections;
- file magic/type/size and archive-bound controls;
- non-execution/sanitization of HTML, Office macros, PDF embedded content, archives, scripts, and active media;
- prompt-injection isolation and tool/output validation;
- quarantine, content-security finding, and safe-preview boundaries;
- least privilege, source-data/secret separation, credential redaction, and environment isolation;
- owner authentication proportional to risk;
- auditability for high-impact rights/security/curation actions;
- conservative rights/attribution/takedown propagation;
- no learner data and minimal incidental personal-data retention;
- export/deletion/retention mechanics where applicable;
- supply-chain/dependency/image/model artifact governance;
- backup/restore and deletion proof;
- incident response proportional to a personal project;
- explicit triggers before public submissions, multiple operators, commercial reuse, or paid/private crawling can be added.

Do not claim legal compliance or complete malware safety without evidence. Define technical controls, remaining risk, and when legal/security expertise becomes necessary.
